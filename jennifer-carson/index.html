Selected work

Behind the numbers.

Growth & acquisition

Turning interest into accounts.

At Wellby, new-member acquisition rose 29.32% year over year in 2024, while Savings Plus Bonus acquisition grew 201.10%. A Q1 2026 referral initiative generated 443 checking accounts and about $1.24M in core deposits.

Next Best Offer personalization influenced 1,627 funded applications representing about $52.7M in associated application value.

Channel performance

Reaching more people for less.

Home equity line of credit conversions increased 49% as acquisition cost fell 20%. Paid-social conversions rose 509% while cost per conversion fell 66%.

Against a September 2025 baseline, non-brand page-one search rankings grew 165% and positions 1–3 grew 179%. Annual search-ad savings exceeded $91K as conversions increased.

Brand & partnerships

A new name with Houston roots.

I led the rebrand from JSC Federal Credit Union to Wellby Financial, from the name and logo to brand guidelines, purpose, PR, and stakeholder communications. The brand carried its NASA and Johnson Space Center legacy into a wider Greater Houston market.

I also helped secure the multimillion-dollar, multiyear partnership naming Wellby the Official Credit Union of the Houston Rockets.
